# Islamic content rules (non-negotiable)

Full policy: `docs/ISLAMIC_CONTENT_POLICY.md`.

- Never write, invent or "recall" Quran verses, Hadith, duas, Arabic text, transliterations or translations as real content. Placeholders must be marked `isPlaceholder: true` and must be impossible to publish.
- Arabic Quran text is imported from a verified source registry, never typed by hand.
- Every religious item needs a source reference, an age group, a difficulty, a reviewer, a review status and a version.
- Statuses: Draft, Pending Review, Approved, Rejected, Archived. Only Approved content can be published. Editing approved religious content starts a new version that needs review again.
- AI may draft. AI cannot approve, verify a reference, or publish religious content. Tag AI-assisted drafts.
- Never depict any Prophet, the Companions or Allah in any illustration, animation or description.
- Use respectful terms and honorifics (for example Prophet Muhammad ﷺ).
- Simplify wording, never meaning. Quiz questions are self-contained and unambiguous. Teach that Muslims believe in all of Allah's Prophets.
- Avoid disputed jurisprudential details for preschoolers. No fear-based or punishment-based religious messaging.
- Label adult obligations (for example Jummah attendance) as "with family, grown-ups decide".
- Automated publish gate must block: missing sources, incomplete references, unapproved content, invalid quiz answers, duplicate questions, bad metadata.
