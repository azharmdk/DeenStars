# DeenStars – Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Status: DRAFT for approval. Version 0.1.

These are structural wireframes (layout, hierarchy, interaction), not final visuals. Illustrations are placeholders. Prophets are never depicted. Mascot direction is open decision D-4.

Frame: iPhone 15 (393×852 pt). Child screens use at least 60 pt touch targets.
